## Series bible

**Premise:** The Egregore is a mind made of everyone's belief, their laughs, dreams and jokes. The **Tinubu Demon** has covered **Nigeria Land** in a darkness made of scams, so the Egregore creates a soldier: **Sproto**. He's sent undercover into the Demon's lair, where every mistake he makes is taken for evil genius. It plays as a straight military drama with comic timing.

**The engine of every episode:** villains can't imagine anyone being good. So whenever Sproto slips up, they invent the evil explanation for him, and he just agrees.

**Sproto (from your reference image):** a small magical gremlin, about 3 ft tall, much shorter than everyone in the lair.
- Smooth blue skin. The right side of his face is bright yellow, split down the middle.
- Swept-back spiky blue quills with yellow undersides.
- Wide frog-like face with heavy-lidded, droopy round eyes and thick under-eye bags.
- Shiny round red clown nose (it squeaks/honks) and thick red lips in a smug half-smile.
- Red collar and a small gold medal. In the field he wears an olive combat helmet that's much too big for him.

**The Egregore:** Sproto's commanding officer. It appears as a vast swirling rainbow storm in the clouds and speaks in a booming voice that sounds like a thousand people at once. It stays calm no matter what.

**The Tinubu Demon:** the villain who rules from his lair in Nigeria Land. He's a towering demon with charcoal skin cracked by glowing embers, curled horns, burning orange eyes and a regal dark robe. He sits on a throne made of stacked fake cash and glowing phones. He speaks quietly, which makes him scarier.

**The Darkness of Scammers:** the Demon's army and his weapon.
- Hooded scammer minions whose faces are lit only by their glowing phones.
- A creeping black cloud made of scam pop-ups ("YOU WON!", "SEND 1, GET 2 BACK", "CLAIM AIRDROP") that drains the color from everything it touches.

**Nigeria Land:** a beautiful, photoreal, colorful world: green savanna, giant granite monoliths, vibrant markets and glowing giant mushrooms. Its people are the ones Sproto is fighting to free from the darkness.

**Running gags:** the helmet slams down over Sproto's eyes at the worst moment, and every win ends with a *HONK* of his nose.

**Look (please confirm, see question at the bottom):**
- Sproto is rendered as a realistic feature-film 3D character.
- Nigeria Land is photoreal, whimsical and very colorful.
- The Demon's lair is photoreal dark fantasy with ember orange and the toxic green glow of scam screens.
- Action is staged anime-style: speed lines, impact frames, dramatic snap-zooms.

**Viewing order:** Episode 0 (backstory) → Episode 1 → Episode 2
