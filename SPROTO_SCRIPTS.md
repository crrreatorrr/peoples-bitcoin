## Series bible

**Premise:** The Egregore is a mind made of everyone's belief, their laughs, dreams and jokes. When the Villains start draining the world of color, it creates a soldier: **Sproto**. He's sent undercover into the Villain Legion, where every mistake he makes is taken for evil genius. It plays as a straight military drama with comic timing.

**Sproto (from your reference image):** a small magical gremlin, about 3 ft tall, much shorter than the hulking villains around him.
- Smooth blue skin. The right side of his face is bright yellow, split down the middle.
- Swept-back spiky blue quills with yellow undersides.
- Wide frog-like face with heavy-lidded, droopy round eyes and thick under-eye bags.
- Shiny round red clown nose (it squeaks/honks) and thick red lips in a smug half-smile.
- Red collar and a small gold medal. In the field he wears an olive combat helmet that's much too big for him.

**The Egregore:** Sproto's commanding officer. It appears as a vast swirling rainbow storm in the clouds and speaks in a booming voice that sounds like a thousand people at once.

**The Villains:** the Villain Legion, led by **Lord Vexmoor** (towering, horned black armor, glowing violet eyes). He has hulking armored henchmen and a skull-masked recruiter.
